# About Parvis

Parvis is a betting game tracker for recording and analyzing games played among friends and family.

## How to Play

### The rounds

A game is a series of rounds. Round 1 deals one card to each player, round 2 deals two, and so on. A round dealt N cards has exactly N tricks.

### Betting

After looking at your hand, and before any card is played, each player states how many tricks they expect to take. In round N a bet is any number from 0 to N, including 0.

The bets do not have to add up to the number of tricks available.

### Playing the cards

The player who bet highest leads the first card of the round. If two players bet the same, the tie goes to the one nearest the priority mark, counted rightwards from it. The mark moves one seat each round.

Each player then plays one card in turn:

- If you hold a card in the suit that was led, you must play one.
- If you do not, you may play any card.
- Only cards in the suit that was led can win the trick.
- Ace is high, 2 is low.

The highest card in the led suit takes the trick, and its winner leads the next trick. This continues until all cards have been played.

### Scoring

A player scores only by taking exactly the number of tricks they bet.

- Exact: 10 points plus the bet. A bet of 0 scores 10, a bet of 5 scores 15.
- Anything else: 0 points. Taking more than the bet scores the same as taking fewer.

On a paper score sheet a successful bet is written with a 1 in front of it, so a bet of 5 becomes 15, which is the score. A failed bet is crossed out and left as written.

The player with the highest total after the final round wins.
